# FAQ

Real questions people search for about Security+, answered briefly. Facts come from CompTIA's own pages, listed in [Sources](sources.md). Where an answer is our opinion or study advice, it says so.

## Is SY0-701 still valid?

Yes. SY0-701 can be booked until it retires: June 11, 2027 for the English exam, and August 13, 2027 for the Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish and Thai versions. Passing it earns Security+. The newer SY0-801 launched on November 17, 2026, and CompTIA now calls it the recommended version. See [SY0-701 or SY0-801](which-version.md).

## How many questions are on Security+, and how long is it?

A maximum of 90 questions, a mix of multiple-choice and performance-based, in 90 minutes. See the [exam overview](exam-overview.md).

## What is the passing score?

750 on a scale of 100 to 900. It is a scaled score, so it does not convert to a fixed percentage of questions. See [scoring](scoring.md).

## How difficult is the Security+ exam?

CompTIA publishes no pass rates, so nobody can give you an official difficulty figure. In our view, the hard parts are the breadth (five domains, many terms that sound alike) and the scenario wording, where two options are partly right and you must pick the BEST. People with some IT or networking background usually find it much easier than those starting cold. The [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) will show you quickly where you stand.

## How much does the Security+ exam cost?

CompTIA sets the price, and it varies by country and changes from time to time, so check the current price on CompTIA's Security+ page or store before you book. Each attempt is paid in full; there are no free retakes.

## Can I pass Security+ in 3 weeks?

It depends on your starting point. As our guidance: someone who already works in IT and knows networking basics can often be ready in three to four weeks of steady study. Someone new to IT should plan for longer, usually two to three months. Use the [study plans](study-plans.md) and stretch each day into a few days if the material is new.

## Do I need Network+ or A+ first?

No exam is required first. CompTIA recommends Network+ plus two years of security or systems administration experience, but that is a recommendation, not a rule. If terms like subnet, VLAN or DNS are unfamiliar, learn networking basics before or alongside Security+.

## Can I get a job with just a CompTIA Security+?

Security+ is widely listed in job postings for entry-level security and systems roles, and CompTIA maps it to many DoD 8140 work roles (see [DoD and government roles](dod-8140.md)). On its own, though, a certification rarely lands a job, in our view. Employers also look for hands-on skills: home labs, help desk or admin experience, and the ability to explain what you did.

## Is CompTIA Security+ worth it?

For people aiming at security, systems administration or government and contractor roles, it is one of the most commonly requested entry-level certifications, which is why so many people take it. Whether it is worth it for you depends on the jobs you are targeting. Read a dozen postings for the role you want and see how often it appears.

## How long is Security+ valid?

CompTIA certifications like Security+ are renewed on a three-year cycle through the continuing education program. Security+ needs 50 continuing education units (CEUs) in that cycle.

## Which CompTIA certs never expire?

Under CompTIA's continuing education program, A+, Network+ and Security+ all renew on a three-year cycle, so none of these three is a lifetime certification. Check CompTIA's continuing education pages for other certifications.

## Can I take Security+ online?

Yes. You can test at a Pearson VUE test center or online through OnVUE. Online exams are recorded through your webcam and have no scheduled breaks. See the [exam-day checklist](exam-day-checklist.md).

## Are there performance-based questions?

Yes. The exam is a mix of multiple-choice and performance-based questions. CompTIA does not publish how many PBQs you will get or how they are scored. See [performance-based questions and pacing](performance-based-questions.md).

## What happens if I fail?

You can retake it. There is no waiting period between your first and second attempt; before a third or later attempt, you must wait at least 14 calendar days. Your score report lists the objectives linked to questions you got wrong, which is the best place to start your next round of study.

## Is 25 (or 35, or 45) too late to start in cybersecurity?

No. There is no age requirement for Security+ or for security jobs. Many people move into security from other IT roles, or from unrelated careers, at every age. What matters to employers is what you can do.

## Is there an official practice test?

CompTIA sells its own learning products. These notes do not cover them. In this repo, every domain page has original practice items with explanations, and there is a [diagnostic](diagnostic.md).

## Where can I practice for free?

Start with the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) and the practice items on each domain page in this repo. For a timed run in the real format, take the [free Security+ (SY0-701) practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/security-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=security-plus).
