# Study plans: 7 days and 2 days

Two plans: one for the final week before your exam, and one for when the exam is two days away. Both start with the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know where your marks are leaking.

A word of honesty first, as our guidance: seven days is enough to **finish** preparing, not to start from nothing. If most of the topics on the domain pages are new to you, stretch each day of the 7-day plan into a week of study, and keep the order. The plan is built around the weights: the heaviest domains get the most time.

## 7-day plan

Expect 1.5 to 3 hours a day.

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [exam overview](exam-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) under time. Note your two weakest domains, weighted by how much each is worth. |
| 2 | Domain 4 (28%) | Read [Security Operations](domains/4-security-operations.md) in full. Do all 4 items. Write the incident response steps and the MFA factor categories from memory. |
| 3 | Domains 2 and 1 (22% and 12%) | Read [Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations](domains/2-threats-vulnerabilities-and-mitigations.md), then [General Security Concepts](domains/1-general-security-concepts.md). Do both item sets. Make flash cards for malware types, attack indicators and crypto terms. |
| 4 | Domains 5 and 3 (20% and 18%) | Read [Program Management and Oversight](domains/5-security-program-management-and-oversight.md) and [Security Architecture](domains/3-security-architecture.md). Do both item sets. Redo the SLE and ALE worked examples on paper. |
| 5 | First timed run | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free Security+ (SY0-701) practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/security-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=security-plus). Score it by domain. Read [performance-based questions and pacing](performance-based-questions.md). |
| 6 | Fix the weakest heavy domain | Go back to the weakest domain from day 5. Reread its "Traps that cost points" and redo its items without the answers. |
| 7 | Second timed run and rest | Take a second timed test in the morning. Compare domain scores with day 5. Read the [exam-day checklist](exam-day-checklist.md). Stop studying by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

Most of your improvement comes from reviewing misses properly. For each one, answer three questions:

1. **What did I confuse?** For example: detective vs corrective, RTO vs RPO, worm vs virus.
2. **Which word in the stem should have stopped me?** For example: "FIRST", "cannot be patched", "no user action".
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "say whose key before choosing".

Keep the answers in one running list. By day 6, the same few confusions will keep appearing, and fixing those is worth more than doing another hundred new questions.

## 2-day plan

For when the exam is already booked and close.

### Day 1: find and fix the big gaps

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 40 min | Read "Key concepts" in [Domain 4](domains/4-security-operations.md), the heaviest domain, focusing on incident response, identity and email security. |
| 3 | 45 min | Read "Traps that cost points" on all five domain pages, then do the practice items for your two weakest domains. Read every explanation. |
| 4 | 60 to 90 min | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/security-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=security-plus). |
| 5 | 30 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit the exam

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Redo yesterday's misses without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Read [performance-based questions and pacing](performance-based-questions.md) and the [exam-day checklist](exam-day-checklist.md). |
| 3 | 15 min | A light warm-up: 10 mixed items you have not seen recently. Stop if you feel tired. |
| 4 | Exam | Sit the exam rested and on time. |

## What not to do

- **Do not use brain dumps.** CompTIA's policy says people who use "brain dump" material (leaked real exam questions) can have their certifications revoked and be suspended from future testing. Beyond the risk, they teach answers to someone else's questions, not the reasoning you need for yours.
- **Do not study the wrong version.** SY0-801 has different weights and objectives. If you are sitting SY0-701, study the SY0-701 objectives. See [which version](which-version.md).
- **Do not skip Domain 5 because it feels like paperwork.** It is 20% of the exam and full of easy marks once the vocabulary is clear.
- **Do not cram late the night before.** Tired readers miss words like FIRST, NOT and BEST, which is where many marks go.
