# The Security+ SY0-701 exam explained

CompTIA Security+ is a vendor-neutral certification for core security skills. These notes cover the **SY0-701** exam (Security+ V7). One thing to know before anything else: CompTIA launched a newer version, **SY0-801**, on November 17, 2026, and SY0-701 retires in English on June 11, 2027. If you are not sure which to book, read [SY0-701 or SY0-801](which-version.md) first.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Exam code | SY0-701 (Security+ V7) |
| Launched | November 7, 2023 |
| Retires | English: June 11, 2027. Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish and Thai: August 13, 2027 |
| Questions | A maximum of 90 |
| Question types | Multiple-choice and performance-based |
| Time | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 750 on a scale of 100 to 900 |
| Recommended experience | CompTIA Network+ plus two years working in a security or systems administrator role |
| Languages | English, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, Thai |
| Delivery | Pearson VUE test center, or online with OnVUE |
| Renewal | Three-year cycle; 50 continuing education units (CEUs) |

Sources: [CompTIA Security+ V7 page](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v7/), the [official SY0-701 objectives](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/cf25ec24b8a511ef9ecbb69c0f9687be), and the CompTIA policy pages listed in [Sources](sources.md), checked 24 November 2026.

## The five domains and their weights

| Domain | Weight | Roughly how many questions (our estimate) | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 General Security Concepts | 12% | about 10 of up to 90 | [Domain 1](domains/1-general-security-concepts.md) |
| 2.0 Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations | 22% | about 19 | [Domain 2](domains/2-threats-vulnerabilities-and-mitigations.md) |
| 3.0 Security Architecture | 18% | about 16 | [Domain 3](domains/3-security-architecture.md) |
| 4.0 Security Operations | 28% | about 25 | [Domain 4](domains/4-security-operations.md) |
| 5.0 Security Program Management and Oversight | 20% | about 18 | [Domain 5](domains/5-security-program-management-and-oversight.md) |

The weights are CompTIA's. The question counts are our arithmetic (weight times 90, rounded down), because CompTIA publishes only the maximum number of questions, not how many come from each domain.

Two things follow from the weights. Security Operations alone is more than a quarter of the exam, so it deserves the most study time. And General Security Concepts, though only 12%, supplies the vocabulary every other domain uses, so it is the right place to start.

## Who the exam is for

CompTIA recommends Network+ and two years in a security or systems administrator role before you sit Security+. That is a recommendation, not an entry requirement. Many people take Security+ as their first security certification, often after A+ or Network+, to move into roles such as security analyst, SOC analyst or systems administrator. CompTIA also lists Security+ against a range of DoD 8140 work roles; see [Security+ for DoD and government contractor roles](dod-8140.md).

## How the questions work

- **Multiple-choice.** Most questions. Some have one correct answer; others ask you to choose two or more. Read the stem for the number.
- **Performance-based questions (PBQs).** Short hands-on tasks in a simulated environment or a drag-and-drop layout: for example, ordering steps, matching controls to scenarios, or reading logs and choosing an action. They usually take longer than multiple-choice questions. See [Performance-based questions and pacing](performance-based-questions.md).
- **Scenario wording.** Many stems include words like BEST, MOST likely, FIRST or NEXT. More than one option may be partly right; your job is to pick the one that best fits the stated goal or constraint.

## How the objectives document works

CompTIA's objectives document lists every objective (for example "4.8 Explain appropriate incident response activities") with bulleted examples under each. CompTIA notes that these bulleted lists are **not exhaustive**: other examples of technologies, processes or tasks for an objective may appear on the exam. Treat the bullets as the minimum, and understand each idea well enough to apply it to something you have not seen.

Watch the verbs. "Compare and contrast" means you must tell similar things apart. "Explain" and "Summarize" mean you must know what something is and why it matters. "Given a scenario" means you will apply it to a situation, often in a PBQ.

## Booking and taking the exam

You can sit the exam at a Pearson VUE test center or online through OnVUE. Online exams are recorded through your webcam, have no scheduled breaks, and can be rescheduled or cancelled only until the appointment's start time. The full checklist is on the [exam-day page](exam-day-checklist.md).

## Retakes and renewal

- **Retakes.** No waiting period between your first and second attempt. Before a third or later attempt you must wait at least 14 calendar days from your last attempt. Each attempt is paid in full.
- **Renewal.** Security+ is renewed on a three-year cycle through CompTIA's continuing education program, which requires 50 CEUs for Security+.

## Practice the real format

Reading teaches the content; timed practice teaches the pace.
