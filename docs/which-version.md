# SY0-701 or SY0-801: which version to take

CompTIA now offers two versions of Security+. These notes cover **SY0-701**. This page sets out the facts so you can choose with your eyes open.

## The two versions side by side

| | SY0-701 (V7) | SY0-801 (V8) |
|---|---|---|
| Status on CompTIA's site | Retiring version | Recommended version |
| Launched | November 7, 2023 | November 17, 2026 |
| Retires | English June 11, 2027; Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish and Thai August 13, 2027 | Not yet announced |
| Questions | A maximum of 90 | A maximum of 90 |
| Time | 90 minutes | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 750 (100 to 900) | 750 (100 to 900) |
| Languages | English, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, Thai | English |

Sources: [Security+ V7 page](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v7/), [Security+ V8 page](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v8/) and the [Security+ overview page](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/), checked 24 November 2026.

## How the domains changed

| Domain | SY0-701 weight | SY0-801 weight |
|---|---|---|
| General Security Concepts | 12% | 16% |
| Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations (701) / Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks (801) | 22% | 24% |
| Security Architecture | 18% | 19% |
| Security Operations | 28% | 27% |
| Security Program Management and Oversight | 20% | 14% |

The five areas are recognizably the same, but the balance moved: more weight on general concepts and threats, much less on program management. The detailed SY0-801 objectives differ too, so do not assume a 701 study guide covers 801 fully.

## Is SY0-701 still valid?

Yes. SY0-701 is offered until its retirement date, and passing it earns the Security+ certification. Retirement means you can no longer book that exam version. The certification you earn follows CompTIA's normal three-year renewal cycle, like any other Security+ holder.

## Which should you choose?

This is our guidance, not CompTIA's:

- **Choose SY0-701** if you are already well into your preparation, your study materials are written for 701, and you can sit the exam comfortably before June 11, 2027 (or August 13, 2027 for the translated versions). Most prep material on the market today is still written for 701.
- **Choose SY0-801** if you are starting from scratch, you plan to test close to or after the 701 retirement date, or you need the newest content for an employer.
- **Do not split the difference.** Pick one version and study its objectives document. The weights and objective lists are different enough that mixing them wastes time.

If your exam needs a non-English language, note that CompTIA lists SY0-801 in English only at the time of writing.

## What these notes cover

Every domain page, practice item and study plan in this repo follows the SY0-701 objectives. If you choose SY0-801, the [free SY0-701 practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/security-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=security-plus) and these notes are still useful for core concepts, but check every topic against the 801 objectives.
