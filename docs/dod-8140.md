# Security+ for DoD and government contractor roles

A large share of people studying for Security+ are doing it for a job with the US Department of Defense or a defense contractor. This page explains what CompTIA says about that and what to check for your own role.

## What CompTIA lists

On its Security+ V7 page, CompTIA lists DoD 8140 work roles that Security+ relates to, including: cyber defense analyst, incident responder, vulnerability analyst, security control assessor, system administrator, network specialist, systems planner, IT project manager, information security manager and secure software assessor, "and many more".

Source: [CompTIA Security+ V7 page](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v7/), checked 24 November 2026.

## What that means for you

DoD 8140 is the DoD's framework for qualifying its cyber workforce by work role. Whether Security+ alone meets the requirement for a particular job depends on the exact work role and level the position is coded for, and the qualification rules are set by the DoD, not by CompTIA. We were not able to read the DoD's own 8140 pages when we checked, so we do not repeat any specific qualification rules here.

What to do, as our guidance:

1. **Get the work role from the job posting or your hiring office.** Postings for DoD and contractor jobs often name the 8140 work role or the qualification required.
2. **Check the requirement at the source.** The DoD Cyber Exchange at cyber.mil publishes the DoD 8140 program information. Your security office or contracting officer can confirm what applies to your position.
3. **Check the version.** If your employer needs the certification by a set date, plan around the SY0-701 retirement date (English, June 11, 2027) or the newer SY0-801. See [which version](which-version.md).
4. **Plan for renewal.** Security+ renews on a three-year cycle with 50 continuing education units. Government roles that require the certification usually expect it to stay current.

## Study priorities for these roles

The work roles CompTIA lists lean heavily on operations: incident response, vulnerability management, monitoring and access control. Those all sit in [Domain 4: Security Operations](domains/4-security-operations.md), which is also the heaviest domain at 28%. Security control assessor and information security manager roles lean on [Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight](domains/5-security-program-management-and-oversight.md), with its policies, risk and audits.
