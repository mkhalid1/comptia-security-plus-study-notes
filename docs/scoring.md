# How Security+ scoring works

Short version: you get a scaled score from 100 to 900, and you pass SY0-701 with 750 or more. CompTIA does not tell you how many questions you need right, how many you got wrong, or how questions are weighted. Your score report lists the objectives you missed questions on, which is the most useful part.

## The scaled score

CompTIA's help center says its exams use a mathematical algorithm to calculate a **scaled score**. The scaled score is derived from what would otherwise be a percentage score, and passing scores are set through statistical analysis. For SY0-701 the scale runs from 100 to 900 and the passing score is **750**.

Because of the scaling, you cannot turn 750 into "you need 83% right". As we understand it (our reading, not a CompTIA statement), different exam forms can differ slightly in difficulty, and the scaling is how CompTIA makes a pass mean the same thing on every form. CompTIA states that it does not disclose the number of questions required to pass.

## What CompTIA does not publish

- The number of correct answers needed to pass.
- The number of questions you answered incorrectly.
- How performance-based questions are weighted compared with multiple-choice questions.
- Pass rates.

Anything you read about "PBQs are worth X points" or "you need 70%" is guesswork.

## What your score report shows

After the exam you receive a score report. It does not show which questions you got wrong or how many. It does list the **exam objectives associated with questions you answered incorrectly**. That list is a study plan: if you need to retake, start with those objectives, using the matching sections of the [domain guides](exam-overview.md#the-five-domains-and-their-weights).

## Answer every question

An unanswered question cannot earn credit. If you are running out of time, make your best guess on everything left rather than leaving blanks.

## What practice score to aim for

This is our guidance, since CompTIA publishes no conversion from practice scores. Because the real passing point is not published as a percentage, aim to score **comfortably above 80%** on practice items you have not seen before, under time pressure, and to be consistent across domains rather than very strong in one and weak in another. A weak domain with a heavy weight, such as Security Operations at 28%, can sink an otherwise good attempt.

Use the score tables at the end of each domain page to track this, and a full timed run such as the [free Security+ (SY0-701) practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/security-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=security-plus) to check pace.

## If you do not pass

- There is no waiting period between your first and second attempt.
- Before a third or later attempt, you must wait at least 14 calendar days from your last attempt.
- Each attempt is paid in full; CompTIA offers no free retests or retake discounts.
- You cannot retake an exam you have already passed under the same exam code without CompTIA's consent.

Take a day off, then rebuild your plan around the objectives on your score report before booking again. The [study plans](study-plans.md) page explains how to review misses.
