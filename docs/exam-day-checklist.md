# Exam-day checklist

A short list to get you into the exam calm, on time and with nothing left to chance.

## The day before

- Confirm the time, time zone and format (test center or online) in your Pearson VUE booking.
- Check the ID requirements in your booking confirmation and make sure your ID is valid and the name matches your registration.
- Light review only: the "Traps that cost points" lists on the five domain pages, and your own miss log.
- Sleep. A rested reader catches FIRST, NOT and BEST.

## Test center or online

| | Test center | Online (OnVUE) |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive or check in | Arrive early to sign in and store your things | Start the check-in early and follow the steps in your Pearson VUE confirmation |
| Room | Provided | A private, quiet room with a clear desk |
| Recording | Center rules apply | Recorded through your webcam. If you do not want to be recorded, CompTIA advises testing at a center |
| Breaks | Follow the center's rules | No scheduled breaks; the exam is meant to be done in one sitting |
| Changes | Per the center's and Pearson VUE's policy | Reschedule or cancel only until the appointment start time, or you forfeit the exam fee |
| Equipment | Provided | Your own computer; run the system test well before exam day. CompTIA does not allow reusing a laptop or workstation another candidate has already tested on |

Source: [CompTIA candidate testing policies](https://www.comptia.org/en/resources/test-policies/candidate-testing-policies/), checked 24 November 2026.

## In the first minute

- Note the question count and the time remaining shown on screen.
- Write your checkpoints on the notepad or whiteboard: where you should be at 30, 60 and 75 minutes (see [pacing](performance-based-questions.md#a-pacing-plan-for-90-minutes)).
- If the first questions are PBQs, read them, attempt only if short, and flag them.

## During the exam

- Read the last line of each stem first so you know what is being asked, then read the scenario.
- Watch for FIRST, NEXT, BEST, MOST likely, NOT and "choose two".
- Flag and move on after about two minutes on any single multiple-choice question.
- Answer every question before time runs out. A blank cannot earn credit.
- Use the review screen at the end to return to flagged items.

## Afterwards

- You receive a score report. It lists the objectives linked to questions you got wrong, but not the questions themselves.
- If you passed, CompTIA's three-year renewal cycle starts, with 50 CEUs needed for Security+.
- If you did not pass, you may retake straight away for a second attempt; a third or later attempt needs at least 14 calendar days since your last one. Build your next plan from the score report's objective list. See [scoring](scoring.md#if-you-do-not-pass).

## Last practice before the real one

Two or three days before the exam, do one final timed run, such as the [free Security+ (SY0-701) practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/security-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=security-plus), so that the pace feels familiar. Do not take a full test the night before.
