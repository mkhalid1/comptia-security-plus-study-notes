# Sources

Every exam fact in these notes comes from the sources below, all checked on 24 November 2026. If CompTIA changes a page after that date, CompTIA's page wins.

## CompTIA exam pages

- [CompTIA Security+ overview](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/): V8 listed as the recommended version, V7 as the retiring version, V7 retirement dates.
- [CompTIA Security+ V7 (SY0-701)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v7/): launch date, retirement dates, maximum questions, question types, length, passing score and scale, recommended experience, languages, domain weights, DoD 8140 work roles.
- [CompTIA Security+ V8 (SY0-801)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v8/): launch date, format, length, passing score, language and domain weights for the newer version.

## Objectives document

- [CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 V7 exam objectives, document version 7.0](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/cf25ec24b8a511ef9ecbb69c0f9687be): every domain, objective and example list used to build the domain guides, the note that example lists are not exhaustive, the acronym list, and the authorized materials (brain dump) policy.

## CompTIA policies and help pages

- [How Are CompTIA Exams Scored?](https://help.comptia.org/hc/en-us/articles/11186025660308-How-Are-CompTIA-Exams-Scored): scaled scores, what CompTIA does not disclose, and what the score report contains.
- [CompTIA Certification Retake Policy](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/test-policies/comptia-certification-retake-policy/): waiting periods, paying for each attempt, retaking a passed exam.
- [Candidate testing policies](https://www.comptia.org/en/resources/test-policies/candidate-testing-policies/): online testing (recording, no scheduled breaks, rescheduling until start time, no shared devices) and score reports.
- [Earn continuing education units](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/ce/learn/earn-continuing-education-units-ceus/): three-year renewal cycle and CEUs required (Security+ 50).

## Search data

- The FAQ questions come from Google "People also ask" results for "comptia security+ exam" and "security+ sy0-701" (United States, English), collected through DataForSEO on 24 November 2026.

## What is our own guidance

The following are our advice, not CompTIA statements: the estimated number of questions per domain (weight times 90, rounded down), the pacing plan, the methods and traps on each domain page, the study plans, which version to choose, practice score targets, and study-time estimates.
